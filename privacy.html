Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. What we collect

When you use CiviAI Campus, we collect:

  • Account information — name, email, password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text), institution, department, and matric number.
  • Reports — the issue description, category, location, and any photo you attach.
  • Chat messages — conversations you have with the CiviAI copilot.
  • Debate and newsroom activity — posts, comments, and articles you submit (if you're a journalist or SUG officer).
  • Profile photo — if you choose to upload one.

2. Who can see your reports

Reports are anonymous to the public — other students never see who submitted a report. However, your identity is visible to SUG officers and institution admins reviewing reports, so they can follow up or notify you about updates. It is not anonymous within the platform's administration.

3. Third parties we use

We share limited data with the following services to run the app:

  • Cloudinary — hosts photos you upload (reports and profile pictures). Cloudinary processes and stores these images on our behalf.
  • Sentry — receives technical error reports when something breaks, to help us fix bugs. This does not include your password or report content by default.

We do not sell your data to advertisers or any other third party.

Under review

4. How long we keep your data

We don't yet have a fixed retention period — data is currently kept for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the platform. We're working to define specific limits, and this section will be updated once that's finalized. If you'd like your data deleted sooner, contact us (see below) and we'll handle it manually in the meantime.

5. How we protect your data

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — we never store or see your actual password.
  • Login is protected against automated guessing attempts (rate limiting).
  • Access to the app's data is restricted to your institution — you only see content from your own campus.

6. Your rights

You can request to:

  • See what data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data

To make a request, email us at samuelkuteyi7@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of CiviAI Campus after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out to samuelkuteyi7@gmail.com.
